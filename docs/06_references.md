## Research limitations and reproducibility

- No inference, training, camera recording or physical sorting experiment occurred. All local performance figures are targets or future measurements; publisher metrics retain their benchmark names.
- The seven deliverables contain recommendations, not a certified legal rule database. Institution/collector acceptance and final amendment status must be confirmed before activating a site profile.
- Direct browsing was intermittent for some ministry URLs, individual TorchVision generated pages, Kaggle and Zenodo. Official source code or indexed official article text supplied the stated verified facts; unavailable fields remain marked unknown.
- Links to `main`, `stable` and living pages are not immutable revisions. Phase 2 must pin dependency versions, model checksums, dataset revisions and reviewed rule snapshots before evaluating reproducibility.
- No large datasets, weights, paid services or external accounts were used. TACO annotation metadata and small source-code text were read only; no images were downloaded.

The follow-up changes the primary recommendation from whole-image classification to detection plus per-instance rules and a batch gate. Scene-completeness checks, rejection priorities, sample sizes, timing and final-recheck design are engineering proposals requiring local validation. Existing dataset licenses and Korean locality restrictions still apply. No application, tracking experiment or training was run for this update.

## User-directed uncertainty policy revision

The latest follow-up changes classification uncertainty from rejection to UNSURE/general and places confirmed category conflicts first. This is a user-defined operational policy, not a claim from the cited scientific or Korean regulatory sources. Existing references, official item labels and known special-stream exclusions remain unchanged. Detection/occlusion ambiguity is separately reported with no command. The architecture documents the current policy, configurable thresholds, warnings and evaluation of fallback errors.
